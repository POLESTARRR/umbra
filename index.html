Why the index
describes nobody

One number is published for a body made of surfaces that face every direction. The gap between that number and your skin is where the whole project lives.

Plane

The index is defined for a horizontal surface. A face is vertical, a nose points forward, an arm faces the car window. None of them receive what the forecast reports.

0° tilt
Diffuse

Rayleigh scattering goes as the fourth power of wavelength. At 300 nm the entire sky dome is a source, not just the sun disc, so shade works far less well than it looks.

40–60%
Density

Sun protection factor is certified at 2 mg/cm². Measured application sits near 0.8, and the penalty is exponential rather than linear. The quantity error dwarfs the timing error.

50 → 4.8
Clock

“Reapply every two hours” is a single proxy for three unrelated failures: under-dosing at the start, film lost to sweat and water, and photodegradation of the filter itself.

3 causes
Shadow

A shadow shorter than your height puts the sun inside 45°, which the model prices at index 5.4 under 300 Dobson units. The folk rule was never folklore. It was a measurement.

The clear-sky model is a ceiling: it carries no aerosol term, so it reads above a published forecast, which folds in cloud and haze climatology. Closing that residual against archive data is the whole of Phase 00.

What it is
made of

Five parts, all of which fit in a file you can read in an afternoon. Nothing here is bought and nothing here phones home.

Position

The NOAA low-precision solar algorithm. Declination, equation of time and hour angle from the timestamp alone. It reproduces the equation-of-time extremes to a tenth of a minute.

±0.01°
Ozone

Climatological total column by latitude band and month, interpolated between band centres. Shipped inside the file, which removes the last reason to make a request.

0.7 KB
Clear sky

Twelve and a half, times the cosine of the zenith raised to 2.42, times the ozone ratio raised to −1.23. Corrected for earth–sun distance, elevation and ground albedo.

μ₀^2.42
Cloud

Camera exposure metadata read as a photometer. Measured luminance over the luminance the model already predicts gives a cloud factor, with no sensor and no fee.

N²K / tS
Store

The reader's dose ledger is written to the device and stays there. There is no account, because there is nowhere for an account to live.

The instrument is thirteen kilobytes of trigonometry.

Phase 00 built the engine. Phases 01 and 02 built the dose ledger and the decay model on top of it, and both are live in the app: it takes your skin type and your body, tells you how many grams to actually use, and then tracks a protection level that falls as you sweat, swim and towel off. Phases 03 through 05 add the anatomical map, the camera photometer and the inference loop.
